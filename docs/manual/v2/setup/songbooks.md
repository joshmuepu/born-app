# Songbooks & import

BORN ships with a large bundled library of songs ready to search, with no setup
needed. A **songbook** is a labeled collection you can add on top of that — useful
for a church's own custom set, or a second language's hymnal, kept separate from
the bundled library and from each other.

## Adding a songbook

In the **Songs** tab, open the songbook menu (top of the list) and click the **+**
to add one — give it a name and it becomes a selectable collection, both in the
Songs search and as a per-channel choice (see [Channels & a second
language](channels.md)).

## Importing songs

Click **Import…** in the Songs tab:

- **From a file…** — opens a file picker accepting ProPresenter 7 (`.pro`,
  `.pro7`), OpenLyrics/OpenSong (`.xml`), ChordPro (`.cho`, `.crd`, `.chordpro`,
  `.chopro`), plain text (`.txt`), Word documents (`.docx`), and PDF (`.pdf`).
- **Paste text…** — paste lyrics directly and BORN will split it into
  verses/chorus/sections for you to confirm.

Imported songs are marked **Imported** in the results list so you can tell them
apart from the bundled library at a glance.

**How to know it worked:** the imported song appears in a search for its title,
with its verses/chorus labelled and ready to project the same as any bundled song.
