# Preview, Live, and saving time

## Preview and Live

At the bottom of the queue panel are two small pictures of the screen:

- **Preview** (dashed frame, left) — what will go up next. By default that is what **Next**
  would show. Click a row in the queue and that item is **staged** here instead (the
  frame turns solid purple); the small **×** unstages it.
- **Live** (red frame, right) — what is on the screen right now. It is read back from the
  screen itself, not from what the app *thinks* it sent, and it flashes when something new
  goes up. A red dot and the word **LIVE** mean something is showing; **Hidden** means the
  screen is blacked out.

**Show** puts the staged item up (or, with nothing staged, does what **Next** does).
**Hide screen** blacks the screen out and keeps your place (it is the same as pressing
**Esc**); it becomes **Show screen**. **Clear** (on a second channel) removes that
channel's text.

Staging never changes what the congregation sees. Only **Show**, **Next**
or **Back** do.

If you have more than one channel, tabs above the pictures let you look at each one. A
channel that follows Main says so under the pictures, and its destinations
(Congregation, Stage, Graphics) show a dot for connected or not.

## Make the queue wider (or narrower)

Drag the thin edge between the browse area and the queue panel, or click it and use the
**←** / **→** keys (**Shift** for bigger steps, **Home** / **End** for the limits, **Enter** to
go back to the default). The width is remembered, and the Preview and Live pictures grow
with it.

## Bigger interface on a big screen

On a large monitor (2560 pixels wide and up) Born Again makes its own controls larger
automatically; the projection output is never affected. To choose a size yourself, open the **⚙** menu at the top right and set **Interface size** (or
press **Ctrl+K** / **⌘K** and pick **Interface size: …**).

## Undo

Removing a queue item, moving one, starting a new service, opening a service file or
dropping something into the queue shows a toast at the bottom with **Undo** and a shrinking
bar. Click **Undo** or press **Ctrl+Z** (**⌘Z**) before it goes. Hovering over the toast
pauses the countdown. Undo only brings back what you removed or replaced; it never changes what is on the
screen right now. If something is on the screen, **File → New service** still asks first.

## Drag it in

Drag a sermon paragraph, a Bible verse or a song onto the queue — a purple line shows
where it will land. Drag a queue row to reorder it. Without a mouse: focus a queue row
and press **Alt+↑** / **Alt+↓**, or right-click (or press the **Menu** key) on anything and
choose **Add next in queue**, which places it right after what is live. Dropping never
puts anything on the screen.

## Right-click menus

Right-click (or press the **Menu** key / **Shift+F10** on a focused item) on a paragraph,
verse, song or queue row for: Show now, Add to queue, Add next in queue, Favorite, Copy
reference, and Open. Nothing is highlighted when the menu opens, so a stray Enter does
nothing. **Esc** closes the menu (and does not black out the screen).

## Favorites and recents

Click **Saved** on the left rail. **Favorites** holds what you have starred; **Recent**
shows the sermons, verses and songs you used lately. Clicking one opens it — it does not
put it on the screen — and **+ Queue** adds it.

## Back and forward

The **‹ ›** buttons at the top (or **Alt+←** / **Alt+→**, or your mouse's back and forward
buttons) take you back through the sermons, chapters and songs you opened. Going back
only opens things; it never projects.

## Search everything

**Ctrl+K** (**⌘K**) opens one box that searches sermons, the Bible and songs, and runs
actions such as **Open projection**. It only opens things; it never puts them on the
screen.

## Pictures

Pictures and slideshows work the same way: staging one in **Preview** changes nothing; only **Show**, **Next** or **Back**
does. Preview and Live show the picture (and, for text, the background behind it). If a picture's file is missing when you take it,
the screen is left as it was. See [Pictures on the screen](media-on-screen.md).
