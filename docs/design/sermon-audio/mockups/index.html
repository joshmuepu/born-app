Tape service — clickable mockups

The sermon text follows the tape. These are design mockups for Born Again (not built yet). The interactive ones run a pretend tape at 12× speed: press Play (or Space), click a paragraph to jump, switch the theme. All text is public-domain Bible text; there is no sermon text and no audio.

Where does the tape live?

The owner asked to see alternatives to having the audio player inside the Sermons section. Five clickable, full-app mockups (today + four options) and a side-by-side comparison. Start with the comparison, then open each option and press the buttons above the app. Recommended: A. Read 05 · placement review and 06 · placement proposal.

12 · Where does the tape live? · Today

The current build for comparison. Use the buttons above the app: go to Songs mid-tape and the tape controls disappear.

13 · Option A (recommended) · Now-playing bar + Tape place

A transport bar at the bottom of every section and a “Tape” place in the rail for recordings, follow, timing and options. The reader stays a reader.

14 · Option B · Tape service workspace + mini-bar

A focused workspace the operator enters from a sermon; only a small mini-bar elsewhere.

15 · Option C · Docked tape panel

A right-hand dock beside the queue, toggled with a button or T from any section.

16 · Option D · The tape as an item in the service plan

The tape is a block in the order of service; its controls live on its row, in the plan that is visible everywhere.

17 · Side by side

The same moment in every option, with ratings against the criteria that matter in a service.

Stopped, playing, paused, stalled, sound output lost, paused for a video, no timing; and the empty Tape place.

Every recording, its status, and where to add, time or fix it.

The tape service itself

01 · Operator: sermon reader with follow-along

The reader highlights the paragraph being spoken and glides to it. Click any paragraph to jump the tape; Space plays/pauses; the "Reader follows" switch frees you to browse without losing your place. Dark / light / high-contrast.

02 · Attaching audio to a sermon

The "Play options" equivalent: a file on this computer (recommended), get the file first, streaming (not yet), and a plain preflight of the chosen file.

03 · Projector view 1: highlight

A screenful of text, current paragraph bright with a large number, the rest dimmed, auto-scrolling. Themes switchable.

04 · Projector view 2: one paragraph at a time

Auto-advance with the "show next early" lead and a countdown bar.

05 · Lower third (OBS) and stage monitor

The livestream lower third and the stage monitor with time, now/next and the sync pill.

06 · Languages

Text vs tape language, English tape with translated text, mismatch warning, and side-by-side two-language projection (including right-to-left).

07 · Sync editor

Tap to sync with a timeline: press Space while the demo plays to place the next marker; undo; nudge; "no text here"; "tape differs"; review of low-confidence proposals.

08 · Phone remote

Tape controls with a paragraph list that follows; a confirmation before jumping; the screen after Blank.

09 · Errors and warnings

Eight situations, each in plain words with one button.

10 · Settings

Lead time, follow style, highlight style, speed range, output device, auto-resume, safety.

11 · Sunday storyboard

Before the service, start, mid-service, end.
