Problem. The header holds 13 controls of four different kinds in one flat row: navigation, search, appearance (theme, contrast), help, and the live controls. Help, Shortcuts, theme and contrast are used once a month; Hide screen, Message and Close projection are used every service, yet look the same. Benefit. Faster to find the screens controls; no wrapping at 1040 px even with the screens live; fewer wrong clicks on a rarely used control; room to add things later without another overflow.

P-02 · One vocabulary for sending something to the screens

Effort SRisk lowImpact high

"Project", "Take", "Put on the screen", "Show", "Hide screen", "Blank" and "Clear" are used for overlapping things. Pick one word per job and use it on every surface.

“Project” and “Take” are retired from the UI; the command palette still finds “Show” when you type “project”.

Problem. The same action is called Project (verse buttons, search results), Take next (live box), "Put verse 1 on the screen" (accessible name), "Tap a verse to put it on screen" (hint), Show (Tape: "Put on the screens"), and the phone says Next/Blank. Hiding is Hide screen / Show screen / Blank / Clear / "Screen hidden" / Esc, depending on where you are. A volunteer who has used the app twice cannot tell whether "Clear" removes the queue item or the live text.

Change. A short glossary used everywhere (operator app, phone, Help, manual, tooltips): SHOW = send to the screens now; NEXT / BACK = step through the queue; HIDE SCREEN / SHOW SCREEN = the blackout (Esc); CLEAR = remove the text but keep the screens on; PROJECTION = the window (Open / Close). "Project" and "Take" disappear from the UI.

Benefit. One word to learn per job; the manual and the phone match the app; fewer mistakes about what a button does.

P-04 · Left rail: Find / Tape / Service groups and one selection colour

Effort SRisk lowImpact med

Eight rail buttons in one list, each section highlighted in its own colour (green, orange, teal, white, plum). Group them and use one meaning for colour.

Bible green, Songs orange, Media teal, Queue plum, Tape white.

Three groups, one selection colour; Saved/Open move into File ▾ (P-03).

Problem. Selected Bible is green, Songs orange, Media teal, Tape white, Queue plum, Saved and Open plain. Colour is supposed to carry meaning (red = live only, plum = the action colour, blue = screens), so five accent colours that mean nothing but "this section" dilute it. Saved and Open are service-file actions, not places to browse, but sit among the places. On a short window the last buttons fell off the bottom (fixed as a bug: the rail now scrolls).

Change. Three labelled groups: FIND (Sermons, Bible, Songs, Media), LISTEN (Tape), SERVICE (Queue with its count, Saved, Open moves into File ▾ per P-03). Selected state is one plum wash for all; the section keeps its own colour only inside its own panel (a small stripe), where it helps recognition.

Benefit. The rail reads at a glance; colour regains its meaning; fewer controls to scan.

P-08 · Rows: one click selects, one key shows; actions appear on hover or focus

Effort MRisk medImpact med

Every Bible verse and sermon paragraph carries "+ Queue" and "Project" buttons and also says "Tap a verse to put it on screen". Choose one gesture.

Click selects, Enter shows, actions appear on the selected / hovered row.

Problem. Each row has three ways to send it (click the row, Project, Enter) and a fourth to queue (+ Queue, Q). The result: a dense column of repeated buttons (24 verse rows × 2 buttons), large body text that shows only two verses at 1366×768, and uncertainty about whether a click shows or selects.

Benefit. Cleaner reading surface; the same gesture everywhere; fewer accidental sends (the single click no longer puts text live).

P-09 · Bigger targets where it matters (Fitts) and a minimum size everywhere

Effort SRisk lowImpact med

The measured controls under 24 px (Help, Shortcuts, subtabs, "Add pictures", filters) become at least 32 px; Show, Hide screen, Play and Panic at least 40 px.

Measured today at 1366×768:

Help / Open projection 22px Help / Open projection 28px

Proposed minimums:

Normal / Show-Hide-Play 32px Normal / Show-Hide-Play 40px

Problem. A DOM probe at 1366×768 found, per section, 10–15 visible controls under 24 px high and 19–28 under 32 px (of 32–46 visible): btn-sm, subtabs, Help, filters. Open/Close projection and Hide screen, the controls that matter most under pressure, are only 28 px high. The phone remote already meets 44 px.

Change. A target-size token (min 32 desktop, 40 for live-critical, 44 on touch) applied to btn-sm, subtabs and links; Show, Hide screen, Play get the larger tier. No visual redesign; padding only.

Benefit. Fewer mis-clicks on a trackpad in a dark booth; easier on touch laptops.

P-10 · Skip links, landmarks and a content-first tab order

Effort SRisk lowImpact med

Reaching the first control of a section takes 26 Tab presses; there is no skip link and no heading level 1.

Tab order today (Sermons): Filters ← footer · logo · Back · Forward · Search · theme · contrast · Help · Shortcuts · Hide screen · Message · Screens · Remote · Close projection · Sermons · Bible · Songs · Media · Tape · Saved · Open · Queue · Search · Date · Series · Location · Recently Played · the search box (stop 27).

Skip to content Skip to the queue Skip to the live controls

First Tab stop: Skip to content (visible on focus). F6 cycles Header → Rail → Content → Queue → Dock. One h1 per section.

Problem. Keyboard-only walkthrough: Tab starts in the header (13 stops) and the rail (8 stops) and the section tabs (5) before the content. There is no "skip to content", no h1, the queue pane is not a landmark, and Tab does not reach the queue pane from the content without crossing everything again.

Change. Skip links ("Skip to content", "Skip to the queue", "Skip to the live controls") visible on focus; one h1 per section; the queue pane as a complementary landmark; F6 cycles the main regions (header, rail, content, queue, dock).

Benefit. Keyboard operators and screen-reader users reach their target in 1–2 keystrokes; the same F6 helps sighted keyboard users.

Effort SRisk lowImpact low

Theme and high contrast are header buttons, the interface size is only in the command palette, and language/fonts are elsewhere. Put them in the ⚙ menu (P-01).

Problem. The interface size (100–175 %) is the one setting that decides whether the app is usable on a small or very large screen and it is found only through the palette ("Interface size…"). Theme and contrast are two separate icon buttons whose icons are not self-explanatory (a sun and a half-filled circle).

Change. ⚙ Appearance: Theme (Dark / Light), Contrast (Normal / High), Interface size (Auto / 100 / 125 / 150 / 175 %) with a live preview line. The two icon buttons leave the header.
