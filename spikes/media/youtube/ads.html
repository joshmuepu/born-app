YouTube ads test

Question: do ads appear in YouTube's embedded player inside this app, and does a YouTube Premium sign-in change that? Run it twice — A. signed out, then B. signed in with a Premium account (steps below). Born Again does not block, skip or hide anything here; it only observes and logs.

  1. Press Play on 3 or more different videos (pick popular music videos; ads are more likely on those). Watch the picture and listen (sound is OFF by default; use the player’s own button if you want it).
  2. After each one, press Ad seen or No ad.
  3. For run B, first press Open Google sign-in, sign in with the Premium account in the window that opens (it may refuse — that is a result too), close it, then repeat steps 1–2.
  4. Press Copy results and paste them back.
